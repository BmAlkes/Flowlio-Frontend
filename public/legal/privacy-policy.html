Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 18, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Flowlio ("we", "our", or "us"). Flowlio is a project management and CRM platform designed for teams and service-based businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when using Flowlio, including:

  • Name and email address
  • Company or organization information
  • User profile preferences
  • Payment and billing information (when applicable)

2.2 Google Calendar Integration

When you connect your Google Calendar account to Flowlio, we access calendar events only to the extent necessary to create, update, and delete project-related events explicitly initiated by you within the platform.

Flowlio does not read, import, or process unrelated personal calendar events, does not access full calendar data, calendar settings, or metadata beyond what is required to manage user-created events.

2.3 Usage Information

We may automatically collect limited usage information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and interaction data to improve platform performance and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain Flowlio
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Create and manage project-related calendar events
  • Process subscriptions and transactions
  • Improve functionality and user experience
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Google Calendar Usage

Flowlio integrates with Google Calendar to allow users to manage project-related scheduling directly within the platform.

Events created in Flowlio are added to the user’s Google Calendar. Google automatically handles synchronization across the user’s devices, including mobile phones and other connected platforms.

Flowlio does not access personal calendar events unrelated to the platform, does not import existing calendar data, and does not access full calendar information.

5. Information Sharing

Flowlio does not sell or rent personal data. Information may be shared only in the following cases:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the security and integrity of the platform
  • With trusted service providers strictly necessary to operate Flowlio

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information, including secure data transmission and access controls.

7. Data Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the service or meet legal requirements.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access and update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for Google Calendar integration
  • Request data portability where applicable

9. Cookies

Flowlio may use cookies and similar technologies to ensure basic functionality and analyze platform usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

10. Third-Party Services

Flowlio integrates with third-party services such as Google OAuth and Google Calendar. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in locations outside your country of residence. Appropriate safeguards are applied where required by law.

12. Children’s Privacy

Flowlio is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
